The Office of the Registrar and Student Records, also known as the Registrar’s Office, supports the University of Central Missouri’s mission to “disseminate knowledge that transforms students into leaders who possess the aptitudes, skills and confidence to succeed.”

The mission of the Registrar’s Office is to support the academic goals of the university and promote student success by providing timely, accurate, quality services to students, faculty, staff, alumni, parents, and other university constituents.

In order to fulfill this mission, the Office of the Registrar & Student Records:

  • Provides secure and confidential maintenance of student academic records in perpetuity.
  • Complies with the mandates of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).
  • Interprets, implements, ensures compliance, and communicates changes regarding academic policies and procedures.
  • Assesses student progression toward timely completion of educational goals.
  • Supports a positive work environment based on integrity, service, and inclusion.

The Office of the Registrar and Student Records supports and abides by the UCM Ethics Policy and Nondiscrimination/Equal Opportunity Statement as set forth by the Board of Governors. 

We also adhere to the best practices and ethical standards adopted by the American Association of Collegiate Registrars and Admissions Officers (AACRAO) and to the Professional Standards for Higher Education as outlined by the Council for the Advancement of Standards in Higher Education (CAS).

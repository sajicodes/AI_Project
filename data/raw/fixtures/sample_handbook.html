Grade Replacement

Under this fixture policy, an undergraduate student may request grade replacement for a course in which a grade of D or F was earned. Only the higher grade is used when computing the fixture GPA after an approved replacement. Graduate students follow a separate graduate catalog rule and are not covered by this handbook section.

Students must submit a grade replacement request to the fixture Registrar before the last day of classes in the term of the retake. A maximum of 9 credit hours may be replaced under this fixture rule.

Academic Probation

Undergraduate students whose cumulative fixture GPA falls below 2.00 are placed on academic probation. Students on probation must meet with an academic advisor within the first two weeks of the following term.

Graduation Application

Candidates for undergraduate degrees must file a graduation application with the fixture Registrar by the published deadline for the intended commencement term. Late applications may delay diploma processing but do not automatically remove a student from the degree audit queue.

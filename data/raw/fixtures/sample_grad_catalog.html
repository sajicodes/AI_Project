Graduate Grade Replacement

Graduate students may replace a grade only with department approval and only for a course in which a grade of C or lower was earned. The original grade remains on the transcript; both attempts appear, and the replacement policy differs from the undergraduate handbook rule.

Graduate Credit Load

Full-time graduate enrollment is defined as 9 credit hours in a fall or spring term under this fixture catalog. Overloads above 12 hours require graduate dean approval.

Thesis Submission

Thesis candidates must submit the final approved thesis to the Graduate School by the published fixture deadline for the graduation term. Failure to meet the deadline may postpone conferral to the next term.
